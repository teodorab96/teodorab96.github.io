About me

I’m a full stack developer with 7+ years of experience building enterprise web applications, mostly in digital banking for major European banks. My focus is Angular and implementing business processes in Camunda BPMN, with hands-on backend experience in .NET and Node.js and additional work in React.

I’ve delivered complete banking journeys – account opening, deposits, loans, overdrafts, investment loans, bank guarantees and leasing – for Raiffeisen Bank in Croatia, Kosovo and the Czech Republic, for both bank customers and the agents who serve them. I care about clean code, good teamwork and learning something new on every project.
