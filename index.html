About me

I’m a software developer with 6+ years of experience delivering enterprise web applications, mostly in digital banking. My focus is the frontend layer in Angular and implementing business processes in Camunda BPMN, and I also work with React, .NET and Node.js.

I’ve delivered complete banking journeys – account opening, deposits, loan origination, bank guarantees and leasing – for both bank customers and the agents who serve them. I care about clean code, good teamwork and learning something new on every project.
